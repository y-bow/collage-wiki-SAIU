The FOSS Club hosted a thought-provoking online session exploring how legal frameworks intersect with collaborative software development.

The discussion unpacked key ideas around copyright in software, clarified what "ownership" means in open source projects, and examined why patenting code is often impractical. Real-world cases such as LinkedIn vs HiQ were used to connect global legal debates with the Indian context.

{/* truncate */}

Students gained clarity on the legal implications of sharing code on platforms like GitHub, and were encouraged to think critically about how law can both protect and shape open collaboration.
