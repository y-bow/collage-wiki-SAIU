The FOSS Club explored the exciting intersection of AI, Computer Science, and Biology through a student-friendly session on AlphaFold and protein folding.

The talk introduced the fundamentals of proteins and the long-standing complexity of the folding problem, traced historical approaches to structure prediction, and dived into AlphaFold's revolutionary deep learning architecture.

{/* truncate */}

Students gained insight into how open research and open tools have accelerated breakthroughs in biology, with real-world impact on drug discovery and scientific research. The session demonstrated how open-source principles enable cross-disciplinary innovation and global scientific progress.
